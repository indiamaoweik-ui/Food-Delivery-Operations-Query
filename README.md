# Food Delivery Operations Analysis
 
## Project Overview                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          

Analyzed food delivery operations using SQL to answer 11 business questions related to revenue, restaurants, food items, riders, customers, cities, delivery times, loyalty tiers, service fees, and complaints.
 
The goal of the analysis was to identify trends, high-performing areas, and operational opportunities that could support business decision-making.

## Business Questions

1. What is the total revenue generated so far?
2. Which top 5 restaurants have the highest ratings?
3. Which food items are ordered the most?
4. What are the top 3 cities generating the highest total revenue?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
5. Which restaurants generated the most revenue?
6. Which riders completed the highest number of deliveries?
7. What is the average delivery time by city?
8. Which loyalty tier contributes the highest revenue?
9. Which restaurants have the highest average service fees?                                       
10. Which restaurants have over 100 complaints?
11. Which customers placed completed orders in Lagos and spent more than ₦50,000 in total?

## Analysis

Used SQL to analyze food delivery data across orders, restaurants, food items, riders, customers, cities, and loyalty tiers. Queries combined aggregation, filtering, grouping, sorting, and table joins to answer business-focused questions and identify patterns in revenue and operational performance.

## Key Insights

- The top-performing city generated a disproportionate share of total revenue, while certain restaurants, menu items, and loyalty tiers consistently outperformed others.
- Cities with longer delivery times, such as Ibadan and Enugu, represented opportunities for operational improvement.

## Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates how SQL can be used to turn operational data into actionable business insights. The findings highlighted differences in revenue performance across cities, restaurants, menu items, and loyalty tiers while also identifying delivery-time patterns that could inform operational improvements.
